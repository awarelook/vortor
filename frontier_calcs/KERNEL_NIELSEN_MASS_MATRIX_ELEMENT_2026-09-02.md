## 1. The central subtlety, stated and resolved first

A mass **spectrum** (eigenvalues) does NOT determine transition **matrix elements** (off-diagonal couplings).
It does so *only if* the same **operator/Lagrangian** that produced the tower is in hand, giving the
eigen**vectors** (mode/soliton profiles) *and* an interaction that couples them. So the whole question reduces
to: **is Nielsen's tower generated by a genuine field operator with an interaction (→ MEs computable in
principle), or is it a numerological mass formula (→ MEs not derivable, firewall stands for cause)?**

The answer is a precise hybrid — **operator-*seeded* but formula-*dressed*, and in any case linear** — which
is why the firewall stands. Three independent findings, each computed or structural:

### 1a. The mass VALUES are a number-theoretic dressing, not the eigenvalue (COMPUTED)
Nielsen's own mass law (TUFT eq. 96–103; reproduced 6/6 to −0.000% in `nielsen_quark_assembly_verify.py`) is
```
  m_{n,s} = Λ₅·(n+1) · exp[ (a₅ + s·λT(n))·n + C₅·n² + b₅·n(n+1)/2 + σ₅·log τ(Kₙ) ] · comp(n)
```
The factor **(n+1)** is the genuine linear Beltrami/Hopf-shell eigenvalue seed (the same "n+1 on S³" FTGB
uses, ground 2 = neutrino). Everything inside `exp[…]` is a **number-theoretic dressing**: `C₅ = ζ(3)/12`,
`b₅ = ζ(5)/8π⁴`, `σ₅ = ζ(3)/16π²`, and a knot invariant `τ(Kₙ) ∈ {1,4,3}` (unknot / Hopf-link /
trefoil Alexander value). Decomposing the mass across the tower (u→t):

| contribution | log(mass ratio) u→t | share |
|---|---|---|
| (n+1) eigenvalue **seed** | 0.693 | **6.1 %** |
| exp[ζ-values + knot] **dressing** | 10.597 | **93.9 %** |
| total (mass spans 80,030×) | 11.290 | 100 % |

**93.9 % of the mass hierarchy lives in the exp-dressing; only 6.1 % is the operator eigenvalue.** The masses
are a *formula riding on an operator-labelled shell index*, not the eigenvalue itself. This is exactly the
project's own honest prior — "masses carry number-theoretic structure that is still CONJECTURAL," the
`exp(a·n − ζ₃n²)` form (frame R55), and the negatively-resolved octahedral-Brillouin gap test (band edges
~n² polynomial ≠ the number-theoretic ratios). **[status: numerology-tier for the VALUES — reject as an ME
source.]**

### 1b. The tower has no nuclear sector (STRUCTURAL)
Nielsen's shells S³/S⁵/S⁷/S⁹ index **generations of fundamental fermions** (leptons, quarks), n=1,2,3. Her
baryon number is the **Pontryagin/instanton** index ∫F∧F, not a Skyrmion degree. The spectrum is indexed by
*generation*, not by baryon number B. **There is no B=2 (deuteron) state and no B=4 (⁴He) state in the tower.**
It cannot represent |d,d⟩ or |⁴He⟩ — there is no space in which to take the overlap. **[status: structural
no-go for this route.]**

### 1c. A linear operator has zero off-diagonal ME (THEOREM)
Even granting `⋆d` as a genuine operator (Nielsen Thm 14: the unique self-adjoint + elliptic + SO(4)-
equivariant first-order operator on coexact 1-forms of S³ — this part is her cleanest, cited result), it is
**linear**. For its own orthonormal eigenbasis {ψ_k}:
```
  ⟨ψ_f | ⋆d | ψ_i⟩ = λ_i · δ_{fi}   →  strictly diagonal, zero off-diagonal.
```
A linear mass operator produces **no transition amplitude by construction**. Off-diagonal MEs require an
**interaction** H_int that is *not* the mass operator — i.e. a **nonlinear field term**. `⋆d` has none: it
fixes eigenVALUES (masses) and free eigenMODES but supplies no coupling between them. **This is the general
reason "spectrum ≠ ME," instantiated here.** [status: rigorous — this is why a spectral operator alone can
never hand over an ME.]

**Verdict (operator-vs-formula).** Nielsen's tower is **operator-seeded (⋆d is real and canonical), linear
(no interaction → no ME), and formula-dressed for its actual mass values (number-theoretic, conjectural),
with no nuclear sector.** On all three counts it cannot supply the LENR nuclear ME. The firewall stands here.
