The electron is a wobble

A high-energy photon bent into a closed ring that chases its own tail — a Hopf knot. It cannot quite close, so it precesses: a permanent, built-in wobble. That wobble is the torsion defect, and it is where charge comes from. Flip its handedness and you have the positron; remove it entirely and you have the neutrino. Charge, mass, and α share one origin: a photon-ring that precesses. The sign of charge is the handedness of the wobble (electron vs positron) — so their masses and spins are equal for free (CPT). Kill the wobble and you get the neutrino: neutral, light, non-precessing. The whirl count is 1/α ≈ 137; expressing charge in mechanical units gives kg·rad/s — spin angular momentum — dissolving the “fake” charge dimension.
