`[V-dim]` units-verified only · `[framework]` a named fringe reading (Reed/Storti/Nielsen/Ginzburg/Greenyer), used as
method/analogy, not endorsed · `[flag]`/`[excised]` a claim quarantined or removed. See `REFERENCES.md` for
